Category 01 · Architecture

Orthogonal Component × Step Matrix & Cross-Assembly Span Connectors

Instead of forcing engineers to hunt through the browser tree to figure out which component owns step 04 or step 11, the 2D Timeline gives the Root Design and every component its own dedicated horizontal lane. Multi-body operations visually bridge their participating component rows with high-contrast vertical span connectors, while the active component is illuminated in a distinctive cyan wash.

📌
Pinned Left Headers & Instant Activation
The left Components column stays anchored during horizontal scrubbing. Clicking any row header activates that component (component.activate), illuminating the row with a cyan wash and routing new features directly to it—eliminating accidental feature authoring in the wrong part.
🔗
Multi-Component Span Connectors
Cross-part operations—such as boolean combines, press-fit assemblies, or fastener joins—render linked chips across every participating component row, joined by high-contrast vertical dotted connector lines and endpoint junction nodes.
·
Configurable Alignment Grid
Unoccupied cells display subtle alignment dots (·) with user-adjustable opacity from 5% to 100%, making horizontal eye-tracking across dozens of chronological modeling steps effortless without adding visual clutter.

Category 02 · DAG Safety

Real-Time Topological DAG Introspection & The Movable Corridor

Reordering features in a parametric B-rep kernel is traditionally a high-friction, trial-and-error operation: drag a feature one step too far and downstream fillets, bores, or boolean cuts fail to rebuild. The 2D Timeline turns the internal Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) into an immediate visual guide the moment any chip is selected, framing the exact safe slot window with green brackets.

▲
Upstream Prerequisites (Ancestors)
Every preceding sketch, datum, or solid operation that the selected feature directly or transitively depends upon is highlighted in warm amber, revealing geometric foundations across all components instantly.
▼
Downstream Dependents (Descendants)
Every succeeding operation or joint that consumes geometry from the selected feature is highlighted in electric blue across all affected component tracks, preventing accidental regression of dependent geometry.
[ ]
The Movable Corridor & Smart Inset
Vivid green boundary brackets frame the exact chronological window—from the last prerequisite to the first dependent—where the action can be ripple-reordered. When coincident with the rollback marker, the bracket automatically insets 3 px inward with zero visual occlusion!

Category 03 · Split Tracks

Asymmetric Split-Track Architecture & Kinematic Joint Scrubbing

Mechanical design doesn't stop at solid bodies—kinematic joints (Rigid, Slider, Revolute, Cylindrical) define how those bodies move together. The 2D Timeline introduces a dedicated Assembly & Joints track (purple accent) alongside Modeling Features (blue accent), separated by an interactive vertical splitter bar.

🟣
First-Class Kinematic Joint Matrix
Each joint (J01, J02, ...) renders connected chips on the two component tracks it constrains, complete with joint type labels, context menu actions (Edit Joint, Drive Joint, Suppress, Delete), joint dependency chains, and an independent assembly rollback marker.
↔️
Fluid Horizontal Track Switching
Drag the central splitter grip to proportion screen width between modeling and assembly history, or click the directional collapse buttons (|< and >|) to contract either track into a compact dock tab for 100% focused workspace.
◫
Dual-Pane Reopen Icon & Snap Physics
When contracted, the splitter tab displays a dual-pane reopen icon (◫) to restore split view with a single click. Snapping thresholds allow smooth drag-to-collapse when dragging towards the window borders.

Category 04 · Dock Adaptation

Adaptive Vertical Dock Sizing: From 1-Row Strip to All-Component Fit

A multi-track timeline should never hold the 3D viewport hostage. Dragging the top resize rail (timeline:top_rail) scales the dock vertically with pixel-exact responsiveness, while double-clicking the rail resets it to default height. The dock adapts fluidly from a 1-row compact strip to a snug all-component fit.

📏
Compact 1-Row Minimum Height (116 px)
Shrinks the dock to a single visible component row with built-in clearance above the horizontal scrollbar and synchronized vertical scrolling on the right—preserving maximum 3D canvas height on laptop screens.
📐
Snug All-Component-Fit Height (264 px)
Expands the dock to fit every component row cleanly from top to bottom with zero wasted vertical space below the final track, eliminating inner vertical scrollbars entirely.
↕️
Top Resize Rail with Quick Reset
Hovering the top border highlights the cyan resize pill with a row-resize cursor. Dragging dynamically resizes the dock, and double-clicking the rail instantly restores the default height.

Category 05 · Feature Organization

Virtual Group Folding, Transitive Suppression & Precision Scrubbing

As a design matures from rapid prototyping to production release, the 2D Timeline provides the organizational and diagnostic tools needed to keep large feature trees clean: virtual group folding across component tracks, automatic transitive suppression cascading, and luminance-matched greyscale rollback states.

📁
Virtual Group Cards & Header Caps
Contiguous steps can be multi-selected (Shift+Click, Cmd/Ctrl+Click) and grouped into named stages. When expanded, Tier 2 banners span the columns; when collapsed, they fold into a single Virtual Group Card spanning all component rows with an integrated header cap and member-count badge.
🚫
Transitive Suppression Cascade
Suppressing a parent feature automatically cascades to all downstream dependents, rendering a 50% opacity slashed-eye overlay (suppress.svg) with a dashed border so the underlying feature icon remains immediately recognizable.
⏱️
Luminance-Matched Greyscale Rollback
Chips to the right of the rollback marker render at 100% opacity in high-contrast greyscale rather than washed-out transparency, keeping icons and facet shading crisp when inspecting rolled-back model states.
